Lemon chickpea soup

Serves four · 35 minutes

  1. Soften a diced onion in olive oil.
  2. Add two cups of cooked chickpeas and vegetable stock.
  3. Simmer for twenty minutes.
  4. Finish with lemon, dill and black pepper.

Roasted carrots

Serves four · 45 minutes

  1. Slice the carrots lengthwise.
  2. Toss with cumin, salt and olive oil.
  3. Roast until the edges brown.
  4. Serve with yogurt and parsley.
